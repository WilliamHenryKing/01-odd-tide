## Cabins, timber and the coastal room

### R01 — Writers House Vega, Norway / Kolman Boye

[Official architect project page](https://kolmanboye.se/project/vega-norway/)

Inspect the exterior, pathway and family-boathouse photographs. The official [works gallery](https://kolmanboye.se/works/) contains a 16-image Vega project section and credits the photographs to Åke E:son Lindman and Kolman Boye. This is evidence for a small timber building meeting irregular exposed rock, with boards, window openings and routes scaled to an actual dwelling. Study the weathering and construction relationships; its sheet-metal roofing does not establish ODD TIDE's ceramic roof design.

Verification: official project text, materials and linked gallery section with image records and captions checked. The direct project page was available through web extraction; one separate HTTP request timed out.

### R02 — Flokehyttene / HOLON

[Official architect project page](https://holon.no/prosjekter/flokehyttene/)

The gallery contains coastal cabin photographs, including a view from inside a compact timber room towards the sea. It demonstrates how a deep window opening, floor edge and human scale can make a small room feel sheltered while retaining the horizon. Use it to inspect Weather House's room reveal and the cabin-to-rock relationship, without adopting the building's triangular plan wholesale.

Verification: official project description and source photo links checked; the `MG_9974web.jpg` interior photograph was also viewed as a small pixel preview. The gallery includes `MG_9784web.jpg` as another inspection target.

### R03 — Manshausen 2.0 / by Stinessen

[Official architect project page](https://bystinessen.com/architecture/manshausen2/)

Inspect the cabins above the rock and sea, including the main photograph credited in its filename to Adrien Giret. These show believable supports, shoreline clearance and an inhabited structure meeting rough natural ground. They are useful for Lantern Lodge's underside and landing, but the later cabins use aluminium cladding: this is a siting and support reference, not proof of the intended grey-green timber finish.

Verification: official project text and photo gallery URLs checked; the main coastal cabin photograph was viewed as a small pixel preview.

### R04 — Manshausen interiors / by Stinessen

[Official architect interior page](https://bystinessen.com/interior-design/manshausen/)

The ten-image gallery accompanies the architect's description of small cabins for two to four people, integrated furniture and a bed withdrawn from full-height glazing. It establishes practical room depth, window reveals and furniture-to-human scale for the roof-open views. Inspect how a few purposeful built-in elements make the room legible, rather than copying its entire furniture arrangement.

Verification: official page and ten linked image records checked; this gallery has not received a full pixel-by-pixel visual review.

## Ceramic roof construction and glaze

### R05 — Residential Terra Cotta Roofing Guide / Ludowici

[Official manufacturer photo and profile guide, PDF](https://ludowici.com/wp-content/uploads/LRT-611-Residential-Brochure-0718.pdf#page=34)

Inspect printed pages 32–38: roof installation photographs, Spanish and Mission tile profiles, the Vintage Green Spanish roof and ridge/trim examples. The guide makes the difference between repeated long ribs and separate overlapping ceramic units concrete: tile length, cover/pan relationships, eaves and ridge transitions must all be readable. Its palette and building styles are examples, not a recommendation to reproduce a Mediterranean villa.

Verification: the 52-page primary PDF, photograph captions and labelled profile sections were checked. Printed page 35 is PDF page 37; printed page 36 is PDF page 38. The PDF viewer screenshot path was inconsistent, so the director should inspect those pages directly before choosing a specific image.

### R06 — Private Residence, Maui / Ludowici

[Official manufacturer project gallery](https://ludowici.com/gallery-study/private-residence-maui/)

Inspect the blue-green glazed roof photographs, especially `Maui-HI-Classic-16-Interlocking-3.jpg` and the wider residence views. They supply a real example of glaze colour variation across separate ceramic units and changing roof planes near bright coastal sky and water. The Classic 16 interlocking form differs from ODD TIDE's proposed rounded courses; use it for glaze response, not as a silently substituted tile profile.

Verification: the manufacturer's live HTML contains seven relevant image records and project captions. Web text extraction failed for this page, but direct HTML retrieval succeeded. A local in-memory preview attempt failed, so no additional pixel-inspection claim is made.

## Rock strata, wet shoreline and tide access

### R07 — Life on the Rocks, Part 1 / Cabrillo National Monument, NPS

[Official park geology article](https://home.nps.gov/cabr/blogs/life-on-the-rocks-part-1-baking-a-geological-cake.htm)

Inspect the photograph of layered sandstone in the Point Loma tidepools, credited to Dr Stephen Schellenberg. It establishes real sedimentary bands with erosion, fractures and irregular exposed edges, a useful countercheck for ODD TIDE's even stacked terrain rings. Take the hierarchy of major shelves and smaller breaks; the fictional island need not reproduce Point Loma's geology exactly.

Verification: official article, embedded photograph record and explicit subject/credit caption checked.

### R08 — A Virtual Visit to the Tidepools / Cabrillo National Monument, NPS

[Official park photographic tidepool visit](https://home.nps.gov/cabr/learn/nature/a-virtual-visit-to-the-tidepools.htm)

Inspect the paired high-tide and low-tide north views, the sandstone steps, the tidepool basin and the low-tide ledges. The series supplies visible evidence of what the same shoreline reveals or covers, and how pools, wet surfaces, surf grass and dry ledges divide the coastal edge. It is directly relevant to the Borrowed Bath stepping-stone payoff and exposed shallows; do not infer real access rules from these reference photographs.

Verification: official page, multiple named photographic records and captions checked, including the high/low north pair credited to NPS/Andrew Rosales.

## Natural coastal planting

### R09 — Plants you can find on the beach / Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew

[Official botanical article with photographs](https://www.kew.org/read-and-watch/plants-beach-uk-coast)

Inspect Kew's own credited photographs of the grass garden, stone pine, gorse and sea thrift. Together they show distinct vegetation structures—fine blades, branching tree canopy, woody scrub and low flowering clumps—rather than a single repeated rounded foliage mass. Use them to establish a coherent plant family and silhouette hierarchy after the director chooses the island's botanical character; this article is not a ready-made species mix for one habitat.

Verification: official page and named image records with RBG Kew/Ellen McHale credits checked. The page also contains third-party photographs; the candidates named here are the institution's own credited images. Separate image fetches timed out, so full-resolution pixel inspection remains pending.

### R10 — The Bloomin' Broom / Cabrillo National Monument, NPS

[Official park plant article](https://home.nps.gov/cabr/blogs/the-bloomin-broom.htm)

Inspect the mature Broom Baccharis beside a path, then the flower and foliage close-ups, all credited to NPS/Andrew Rosales. They demonstrate a leggy branching base, many small foliage ends and broken outer silhouette at both whole-shrub and close scales. The reference can inform clustered coastal scrub and plant-to-path contact; it does not select this Californian species for ODD TIDE.

Verification: official article, mature-plant photograph and five labelled detail photographs checked in the page's image records and captions.

## Outdoor bath construction and practical light

### R11 — Coastal hot-tub photo collection / Skargards

[Official manufacturer press and media gallery](https://www.skargards.com/ie/about-skargards/press-and-media/)

Inspect the rocky-shore tub views, aerial views, and the shore scene with lanterns at dusk. They provide construction evidence for a round outdoor bath: enclosing wall, rim, access steps, stove/flue and a readable relationship to the ground and water. The dusk photographs also offer a scale check for small practical lights; choose the source and falloff, not a generic orange wash over the scene.

Verification: official gallery's descriptive photograph records checked; a coastal tub photo was viewed as a small pixel preview. Its press-use terms are limited and do not authorise shipping the photographs as site assets.

### R12 — Bohemen outdoor bath / Hikki

[Official manufacturer product gallery](https://hikkisweden.com/products/bohemen)

Inspect the top, side, rear and detail photographs. They make bath depth, wall thickness, internal backrests, a floor grate, stove connections and material joints visible—precisely the construction cues missing when a bath reads as a ring around a flat water disc. This product is aluminium and oak; use its functional construction relationships without changing the project's intended bath material by implication.

Verification: the live product page includes explicitly labelled front/rear/side/top/detail image records and a component list. No safety, installation or operating specification is being adopted for the fictional experience.

## Clear sky, overcast coast and dusk luminance

[Official CC0 HDRI page with photographic preview and backplates](https://polyhaven.com/a/cloudy_cliffside_road)

Inspect the panorama and the coastal backplate photographs. They supply an overcast sea-cliff reference for soft sky fill, low-contrast distant water and the relationship between horizon haze and nearby terrain. This is a lighting reference; the road and fence are not proposed island content.

### R14 — Sundowner Overlook / Poly Haven

[Official CC0 HDRI page with photographic preview](https://polyhaven.com/a/sundowner_overlook)

Inspect the clear coastal panorama and its low warm sun against cooler sky. It supplies a real contrast reference for sunlit versus shaded surfaces and for the sea's relation to the sky in clear conditions. Its afternoon direction is a candidate for the apricot state, not a prescription for the 09:00 arrival scene.

Verification: accessible, unlocked source page, panorama preview record and CC0 declaration checked. No HDR file was downloaded or used.

### R15 — Golden Bay / Poly Haven

[Official CC0 HDRI page with photographic preview](https://polyhaven.com/a/golden_bay)

Inspect the waterfront twilight panorama: warm practical lamps, local reflections in calm water and a cooler blue sky. It provides a reference for dusk luminance relationships, with lights reading as small sources and their effects remaining spatially tied to them. The urban waterfront and density of lamps are outside ODD TIDE's subject; only the light relationships are candidates.

Verification: accessible, unlocked source page, photographic preview record and CC0 declaration checked. No HDR file was downloaded or used.
