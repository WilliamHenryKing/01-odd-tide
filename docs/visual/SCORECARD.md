## Three visible remaining flaws per bookmark

### arrival-day

- Repeated shelf-like terrain and blob foliage.
- Island occupies too little of the wide canvas for its detail.
- Flat sea and indoor IBL lack a connected environment.

### arrival-high-water

- Flooding has no convincing shoreline wetting/contact.
- Cliff bands repeat uniformly.
- Sea highlights are drawn patterns rather than scene reflections.

### weather-exterior

- Foliage has repeated faceted pancake forms.
- Roof and facade lack plausible joints and fasteners.
- Glazing and terrain have weak grounding.

### weather-interior

- Raised roof has little hinge/support construction.
- Bed and pillows are smooth generic rounded forms.
- Wood/cloth/roof response lacks reference-scale surface detail.

### nap-observatory

- Dome has broad plastic-looking reflections.
- Interior telescope and furnishings do not hold up close.
- Neighbouring roofs crowd the portrait composition.

### lantern-dusk

- Deck lamps glow without casting local light.
- Boards repeat with near-identical surface response.
- Backdrop/environment still disagree at dusk.

### arrival-night

- Night reads as the daylight scene darkened.
- Bulbs lack convincing light pools.
- Flat sea/sky lacks night depth cues.

### coast-grazing

- Stacked extrusion bands look manufactured.
- Low-poly rocks have stretched speckle, not stone relief.
- Inspection framing collides with public hero text.

### bath-arm-length

- Bath is a torus and bowl without referenced fittings.
- Water looks like a flat coloured disc.
- Inspection framing collides with public hero text.

### horizon

- Hard sky/sea boundary.
- Island has little depth at distance.
- Mobile hero is too small in the available scene area.
