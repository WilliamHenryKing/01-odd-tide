## 6. Three highest-value next fixes for the planting

1. **Shade the cards as foliage, not as two-tone planes.** Darken or translucency-tint the back faces (212 cream blade backs at planting-close; 295 px above L 150 in the dusk spray), bend card normals toward the canopy centre so each tree shades as a volume, and treat the edges (MSAA or alpha-to-coverage; `antialias` is off) so the outlines stop stair-stepping at 2×. This is the change that separates "competent indie" from "premium" for every hero frame at once.
2. **Ground every plant.** Add a root flare and a contact-darkening decal at each trunk base (L 78.5 under the base against 63.5–69.5 beside it), keep sprays out of building volumes (the slab at weather-interior x1135–1290, y325–365) and off cliff faces (nap-observatory x1505–1560, y770–825), and give the stemless cluster at planting-close x610–715, y190–262 a visible stem or a position on the plateau.
3. **Fix distribution and colour.** Even out blade density across the near plateau (10.9 → 3.1 per 100×100 px), replace one-pixel stubble at mid distance with clumps or nothing, pull the fern's albedo toward the turf (78/91/48 against 124/107/76), add a second card family so canopy and scrub do not share one frond, and thicken the far silhouettes (horizon canopy 71 % opaque, BEFORE 97.8 %).
